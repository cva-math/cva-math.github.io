What you just saw

The picture builds the polynomial. A cut of x makes the box x tall, 11 − 2x long and 8½ − 2x wide, and the volume is those three multiplied. But the picture also makes the rules: past a 4¼-inch cut, the corners overlap and there’s no box left. The algebra doesn’t notice. It keeps handing you volumes — negative ones, then positive ones again — for boxes nobody could ever fold.

The name for this, when you’re ready for it: the domain — the inputs that make sense for the situation. The polynomial V(x) = x(11 − 2x)(8½ − 2x) works for any x. The box only works for 0 < x < 4¼.

The algebra will answer anything. Knowing which answers count is up to you.
