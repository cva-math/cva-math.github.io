Stuck at One Percent

Every progress bar is a question with the whole left out. Six minutes in at 30% means the whole update takes twenty minutes. Six minutes in at 1% means something much worse.

Drag the bar. Watch the whole.
Act I The Bar

Six minutes into an update. Drag how far the bar has gotten, and find out how long the whole thing takes.

Say the bar fills at a steady pace — real ones don’t always, but say this one does. The six minutes you’ve waited are whatever percent the bar shows, so the whole update is 100% of that. Drag the bar until the whole update takes an hour. Then two. Then ten.

What you just saw

An hour at 10%. Two hours at 5%. Ten hours at 1%. Every time you halved how far the bar had gotten, the whole update doubled. The six minutes never changed — what changed was how big a piece of the whole they were. When a part is a small percent, the whole behind it is big.

The name for this, when you’re ready for it: the part is so many hundredths of the whole, so the whole = the part × 100 ÷ the percent. Six minutes at 1% is 6 × 100 ÷ 1 = 600 minutes. At 30% it is 6 × 100 ÷ 30 = 20. The percent is what you divide by — which is why shrinking it blows the whole up.

Act II Every Bar at Once

Now leave a dot every time you let go, and see what shape the whole update makes.

Across is how far the bar says it has gotten. Up is how long the whole update must take, for the same six minutes. Drag and let go, again and again — and watch where the dots go as the bar heads toward zero.

Drag and let go. Six dots, then look at the shape.

What you just saw

The dots hug the floor most of the way across — anything past 20% is a short update. Then, as the bar heads toward zero, they climb faster and faster, and at 1% they reach ten hours. The curve never touches the left edge. There is no percent small enough to make it stop climbing.

Every dot is the same fact, 6 × 100 ÷ the percent, read at a different percent. Each problem on this module’s Pages was one dot on a curve like this one — one part, one percent, one whole. Here you can see all of them at once.

Then The Card

One bar the page won’t let you drag to.

The bar that won’t move

Six minutes in, and it still says 0%.

Every percent you tried gave you a whole update, even when it was a very long one. So how long is an update that’s still at 0% after six minutes?

Try to find it. If you can’t, say what’s stopping you.

Six minutes is a big piece of a short update — and almost nothing of a long one.
